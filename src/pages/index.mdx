	## Computers!

	I spend a significant portion of my time writing code and creating things with technology. I believe in having passion for your craft and attention to detail. Even through the most recent, LLM-filled years, I still end up writing most of my code by hand — if I can't do a better job making something than an LLM could, then why bother making it? Since I just adore building things, I have created a copious pile of side projects that I don't really know what to do with, and I'm constantly starting more. You can [see an abridged list of stuff I've done](#projects) if you're interested.

	I use TypeScript, Rust, and React on a daily basis and love them. I'm also a big fan of OCaml and Haskell, although I wish there was a nice hybrid of the two... but I have so far avoided the temptation to make my own language, because another important thing about me is that I'm very practical. I used to daily drive NixOS with KDE Plasma (you can [check out my ancient dotfiles](https://github.com/kognise/dotfiles/)) but now just use macOS and reserve NixOS for my desktop at home. I design everything for myself and for others using Figma.

	I used to often be asked, "hey Lexi, what kind of programming do you do?", and I never quite knew how to answer. I do everything. Frontend! Backend! Embedded hardware! Mobile apps! Compilers! Infrastructure! I find it immensely enjoyable to learn how shit works and plunge down rabbit holes, and I pick up whatever's needed to build what I'm excited about. I get very bored when I have to work in only one area.

	A consistent thread through the past several years of my life has been meeting my online programmer friends in person. I'm lucky enough that many, many friends I previously only knew online now live within a few blocks of me. This chapter of my life began in the summer of 2021 when I rode across the country on a hackathon on a [superdome train](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Dome_(railcar)), and since then, I've run into friends as far as Latvia and Iceland, and in places as strange as Kalamazoo (a real place in Michigan, apparently!).

	## Airplanes!

	I think aviation is pretty cool. Over the pandemic I got really into flight simming and [VATSIM](https://vatsim.net/), and then I realized, hey, I can do this in real life.
	
	I started training for my Private Pilot License, first out of [KSSF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stinson_Municipal_Airport), then [KBTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Leahy_Burlington_International_Airport), and now [KSQL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Carlos_Airport_(California)). I keep getting interrupted (by moving, by broken bones, etc.), but I'm hoping to finish my license soon.

	Throughout my training I've been flying various [Cessna 172](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cessna_172) models from different generations. I pinned a [VFR wall planning chart](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/flight_info/aeronav/productcatalog/planningcharts/vfrwall/) to my wall and I'm sticking pins in every airport I land at, and I'm hoping to visit as many airports in the U.S. as I can; maybe some day I will land in every state! I mentioned travelling earlier: when I have the chance, I also love flying as a passenger and I've spent many, many hours in coach visiting new horizons and making new friends.

	If you want to hang out and don't live in San Francisco (where I live right now), still reach out! I might stop by your city some time!

	## Music!

	When I'm not programming, flying, or slacking off, I'm probably doing something music-related. I've been playing classical, jazz, folk, Balkan, and contemporary music on cello for over 15 years. I briefly played piano, although I was never very good. I also more recently got a 5-string [NS Design](https://thinkns.com/) electric cello, which I've been having a lot of fun with.

	## Other things!

	As a kid, I really didn't enjoy any form of physical activity, but I've been coming around to it and have enjoyed all the beauty you can experience in the world and in yourself through exercise. I already mentioned square dancing, but here are some other things I've been doing:

	* I got into rock climbing in Vermont and have kept it up. I don't like bouldering that much, but I LOVE top roping. I'm hoping to do more outdoor climbing in the next year.
	* I've been getting into Brazilian jiu-jitsu. It's fun sport that's as intellectual as it is strength-based.
	* I wouldn't call myself a runner, but I ran a 15k and that felt pretty cool... some day I might even do a marathon.
    * I bike positively everywhere in San Francisco. It's fun, good exercise, and often much faster than every other form of transport! But it's also just full of joy. The longest I've biked is probably about 150 miles, but I usually just travel commuting distances.
	* I've very recently been learning to sail! It's crazy to me because it is probably one of the oldest hobbies in existence. People have been doing it for thousands of years! But the water is just so freeing and beautiful, so I will happily join the train.

	What else... I really have too many hobbies:
	
	* I love cooking and baking. I'm a fan of the mise en place mindset, and I especially love hosting parties where we cook tons of food and I get to boss around a small army of sous chefs.
	* I enjoy building mediocre furniture and tools out of wood. I have a closet full of woodworking tools in my apartment in San Francisco, which can be jarring to our neighbors.
	* I learned how to solve a Rubik's cube and got it down to 20 seconds, but I haven't practiced as much recently.
	* I've always been fascinated with the law. 	* I also think double-entry accounting is pretty fun, and enjoy doing taxes so much I do my friends' taxes for them. I'll do yours for you if you want! I'm not even joking!
	* This year, I got into a bit of [light Wikipedia editing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User:Kognise) and now I'm proud to have done photography for various places around San Francisco, maintained the [#1 article for a few weeks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_of_the_Clancy_children), and written articles on topics varying from [microform registers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Register_of_Microform_and_Digital_Masters) to [lawsuits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lemmon_v._Snap,_Inc.), [clowns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Baptiste_Dubois_(actor)), and [peppers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cascabella_pepper).
